বাংলা ভাই (Bangla Bhai) — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026 · Operated by Royal Universe · https://banglabhai.dpdns.org

বাংলা ভাই is a Bengali AI assistant web app. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use Google user data, and how you can delete it.

Data we store

  • Login email address and session cookie
  • Your chats, notes and settings (kept separate per account)
  • An audit log of permissions you grant and actions the assistant takes

Google user data

If you choose to connect a Google account, the app requests only these scopes and uses them only for the features you trigger:

  • Gmail (gmail.send / gmail.compose): to send one-time login codes from the owner's account, and to draft or send an email only when you explicitly ask and confirm. We do not read, scan or store your inbox.
  • Google Drive (drive.file): to save or open files you choose in the app. We access only files the app created or you selected.
  • Google Calendar: to show or create events when you ask.

Google user data is never sold, never used for advertising, never used to train AI models, and never shared with third parties except as needed to provide the feature you requested. OAuth tokens are stored encrypted on our server. The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

AI model providers

Chat text may be processed by model APIs (Groq, Google Gemini, OpenRouter) to generate answers. Google account data is not sent to them unless you paste it into a chat yourself.

Data retention

  • Chats and settings: kept until you delete them or your account.
  • Login codes: expire after 10 minutes.
  • OAuth tokens: kept until you disconnect the service in Connections, or revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Audit logs: up to 12 months.

Deletion & your rights

You can export or delete your data in the app. To delete your account and all associated data, email skbadol3210@gmail.com; we complete deletion within 30 days.

Security

HTTPS everywhere; secrets are stored encrypted and never shown in chat.
